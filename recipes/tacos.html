Description

Super easy and tasty street taco recipes! This recipe is customizable with any meat or veggie toppings

Ingredients

  • 1 serving of chicken (or any meat of your choice)
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 2 cups cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup black beans
  • 1 avocado
  • 1/2 lemon

Steps

  1. Grill and chop the chicken according to the recipe instructions
  2. Mash avocado with a fork. Sprinkle salt and pepper. Squeeze lemon juice. Stir
  3. Fill each tortilla with a spread of avocado and a scoop of meat
  4. Add fresh cilantro, onion, beans and cheese on each taco. Finish with lemon juice on top
